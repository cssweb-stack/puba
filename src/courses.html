PUBA Courses

Our curriculum is designed to equip future leaders with the strategic, analytical, and ethical foundations needed to navigate modern public service and drive meaningful social impact. Explore our graduate and undergraduate offerings below, organized by key areas of professional focus to help you map out your academic journey.

Core Foundations & Management

Master the operational, historical, and strategic frameworks essential for leading public and civil service organizations. Coursework in this area focuses on structural organizational theory, human resource and personnel management, public-sector budgeting across government levels, and strategies for organizational reform. Students gain a comprehensive understanding of decision-making dynamics, management challenges, and the administrative tools needed to keep public entities running effectively.

Leadership, Ethics & Collaboration

Build the critical communication, cultural agility, and ethical decision-making skills needed to manage diverse teams and inter-organizational networks. Courses cover practical moral reasoning, inclusive workplace strategies, and cross-cultural leadership for complex public projects. You will also explore real-world leadership dynamics, effective followership, and methods for managing multi-agency collaborations across government and non-profit sectors.

Policy Analysis, Law & Governance

Engage with the mechanisms of policy creation, legal frameworks, and environmental dynamics that influence decision-making at local, national, and global levels. This track delves into economic analyses of U.S. law, political environments, advocacy strategies, and information policy. Students examine global administrative systems as well as indigenous governing frameworks and service delivery models, particularly in the Americas and the Pacific.

Research Methods & Evaluation

Develop data-driven evaluation techniques and rigorous social science methodologies to measure program outcomes and inform public policy. Students learn hands-on quantitative and qualitative approaches to conduct valid field research, establish performance metrics, and translate data into actionable insights for public and non-profit decision-makers.

Nonprofit Sector Specialization

Acquire targeted management, grant-writing, and strategic planning tools customized for the unique landscape of non-profit entities. Coursework covers fundamental non-profit structures, mission scope, resource and volunteer development, and tactical execution skills like advocacy, program evaluation, and strategic business practices.

Applied Learning, Research & Special Topics

Translate classroom theory into real-world action through field placements, capstones, and targeted independent study. This area offers practical internship and practicum opportunities across public, private, and non-profit sectors—including specialized tracks in peace building and non-profit management—culminating in capstone projects and independent research on emerging issues affecting Hawaiʻi and the region.
