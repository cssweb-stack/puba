Support PUBA

The Public Administration Enrichment Fund was established to support the development of the Public Administration Program, its students and faculty, and its public service activities. Support includes funding of research, teaching, hospitality, travel, and any other needs of the department.

Contributions of any size are sincerely appreciated.

Additional Funds

  • Dick Pratt Scholarship - Support exceptional public leadership.
  • Herman S. Doi Memorial Fellowship Fund - Used to promote greater success to public service education for those most likely to make a significant contribution to the State of Hawai'i through work in the public sector.
  • Wood Pacific Island Health Administrators Scholarship - Provides students from Pacific Island nations who are seeking an MPA degree.
