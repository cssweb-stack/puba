PUBA Faculty

Our faculty come from a variety of disciplines, providing students with the opportunity to learn different perspectives about important public issues. Faculty have recently published in Public Administration Review, American Review of Public Administration, Journal of Public Administration Research and Theory, Review of Public Personnel Administration, Publius, and Public Management Review.
