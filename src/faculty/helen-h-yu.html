Background

In 2014, I retired from the Air Force as a Lieutenant Colonel after 20 years of active duty service. The last 16 years were spent in a non-uniform capacity as a Special Agent with the Air Force Office of Special Investigations. I am a subject matter expert in counterintelligence and counter-terrorism, and served on three combat tours in support of Operations Joint Forge, Enduring Freedom, and Iraqi Freedom. My professional experience have influenced every aspect of my academic pursuits, and I hope my efforts build the next generation of public leaders in Hawaiʻi, the continental U.S., and the Asia-Pacific region.

Human resource management and law enforcement.

Research

My research interests include the recruitment, retention, and promotion of women in nontraditional occupations, specifically federal law enforcement, and policy decision-making in policing.

Community Engagement

Since 2015, I've served as a Senior Advisor or Board Member for the Women in Federal Law Enforcement (WIFLE) Foundation, Inc. In this capacity, I provide consultation on the recruitment, retention, and promotion of women in federal, state, and local law enforcement, and education to the criminal justice system and the media of the value women bring to the law enforcement profession. In addition, I'm a Research Partner with 30x30, an initiative to advance the representation and experiences of women in all ranks of policing agencies across the United States.
