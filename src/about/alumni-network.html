Alumni Network

Our large alumni network of nearly 1,000 working professionals in federal, state, and local public and nonprofit organizations, and international government and non-governmental organizations extends through Hawaiʻi, the continental U.S., and dozens of countries worldwide, particularly in the Asia/Pacific.
