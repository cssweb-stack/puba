U.S. News & World Report recently released the 2025 Best Graduate Schools rankings, and we are pleased to announce that the Public Administration Program (PUBA) at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa continues to rank in the top third of all programs nationwide.

According to their report, our PUBA ranking has risen from #89 to #82, demonstrating excellence in our performance as a NASPAA-accredited program.
