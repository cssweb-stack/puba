U.S. News & World Report recently released the 2023 Best Graduate Schools rankings and we are pleased to announce that the Public Administration Program at University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa is in the top third of all programs nationwide.

According to report, our PUBA ranking has risen from #97 to #88, demonstrating excellence in our performance as a NASPAA-accredited program.
