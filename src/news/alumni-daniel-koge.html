Our PUBA alumnus, Daniel Koge, is a Civil Engineer at the Honolulu Board of Water Supply. The Board of Water Supply manages Oahu's municipal water resources and distribution system, providing residents with safe and dependable water service.

In this position, Daniel finds his MPA greatly benefits his position within a local utility where the level of service, public safety, social equity, precedence, resiliency, and private interests are moving targets that must be balanced with engineering judgment. His technical degree and MPA complement each other to navigate this ever-flowing tide of change.

Congratulations, Daniel, on your position at the Honolulu Board of Water Supply, and thank you for your service to our community!
