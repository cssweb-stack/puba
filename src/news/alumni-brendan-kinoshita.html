Fall ‘17 Alumnus Advances to Prominent Role in the Department of the Attorney General.

The Public Administration Department has fantastic news to share. Our PUBA alumnus, Brendan Kinoshita, has been appointed as the Administrative Services Manager for the Department of the Attorney General. The Department is composed of over 180 attorneys and 500 professional support staff spread across the islands.

With this new position, Brendan is responsible for the administrative functions and operations of the largest law firm in the state. He oversees matters in human resources, information technology, budget, fiscal, procurement services, and other departmental policy and instructions.

Congratulations to Brendan on your professional success. We are excited for your new journey ahead.
