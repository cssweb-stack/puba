Funding Opportunities

We offer a variety of funding opportunities to current students. View current tuition costs (in-state, out-of-state).

PUBA Scholarships

PUBA is able to provide limited financial assistance each year. Award criteria include:

  • Both academic merit and financial need
  • For students who are engaged in issues affecting the Hawaiian community
  • For international students
  • For capstone and practicum

Scholarships, accessible through the STAR System, include the D. W. Wood Scholarship for Pacific Island Health Administrators, the Herman S. Doi Memorial Fellowship Fund, the Dick Pratt Scholarship, and the Public Administration Achievement Scholarships.

Please note that receipt of scholarships or stipends can affect your financial aid package amount.

Graduate Assistantships

Graduate assistants (GAs) must maintain full-time status with a minimum of six credits and a maximum of nine credits. All GAs have the option of purchasing health care as an employee of the University of Hawaiʻi. We offer one GA position per year, with preference given to second-year students. PUBA students are welcome to apply for GA positions in other departments.
