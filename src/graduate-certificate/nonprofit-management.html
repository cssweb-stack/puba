Graduate Certificate in Nonprofit Management

Lead with purpose. Create lasting impact.

The Graduate Certificate in Nonprofit Management (15 credits) empowers students and professionals to strengthen their leadership, management, and problem-solving skills in the nonprofit world. Designed for those committed to the public good, the program provides practical tools, ethical frameworks, and strategic insight to navigate the sector’s growing challenges.

Drawing students from disciplines such as social work, law, Hawaiian studies, and business, the certificate fosters collaboration across communities and professions—all united by the goal of building stronger, more resilient nonprofit organizations.

This certificate also serves as an exceptional complement to many Master’s and PhD programs, providing a solid foundation in leadership and organizational management that enhances both academic study and professional advancement.

Program Requirements

In addition to the requirements listed below, students must understand and meet all Graduate Division requirements.

Core Coursework (9 credits)

  • PUBA 604: Leadership and Ethics (3)
    Examines leadership and ethical frameworks in public and nonprofit settings, emphasizing moral reasoning and responsible decision-making.
  • PUBA 630: Nonprofit Management (3)
    Introduces the structure, mission, and management of nonprofit organizations, including governance, funding, and volunteer engagement.
  • PUBA 631: Nonprofit Management Practices and Tools (3)
    Focuses on practical management tools such as grant writing, strategic planning, evaluation, and advocacy.

Elective Coursework (3 credits)

  • One PUBA course (outside courses may be approved by an advisor)

Practicum (3 credits)

  • PUBA 691: Nonprofit Management Practicum (3)
    Apply your learning in a real-world setting through 100 hours of supervised professional experience.

Additional Notes

  • Up to 9 credits earned in this certificate may be applied toward the Master of Public Administration (MPA) or another graduate degree (with approval and restrictions).
  • Students wishing to continue into the MPA program must submit a formal application and be accepted.
  • We offer a variety of funding opportunities to current students.
